The Perfect V60 pour-over — fruity & floral forward
After 3 weeks of dialing in this Ethiopian, here's what really lets the blueberry and jasmine shine.
Coffee Type
☕ V60 Pour-over Espresso Americano French Press AeroPress Chemex
Tools Used
⚙️Comandante C40 🫖Hario V60 ⚖️Acaia Pearl 💧Fellow Stagg EKG
Brewing Process
1
Dose & Ratio
15g coffee / 250g water — 1:16.7 ratio. Rinse your V60 filter with hot water first to remove paper taste.
2
Grind
Medium-fine — 20 clicks on the Comandante C40. Should look like coarse sand. Adjust finer if extraction is too fast.
3
Water Temperature
96°C / 205°F. For lighter roasts, higher temps help extract the delicate floral compounds without astringency.
4
Bloom
Pour 30g of water for 45 seconds. Swirl gently to saturate all grounds. The bloom should double in volume — good sign of freshness.
5
Pour in 3 Stages
Stage 1 (0:45): pour to 120g, slow spiral from center out.
Stage 2 (1:30): pour to 185g, maintain level.
Stage 3 (2:00): final pour to 250g. Total brew time: 3:00–3:15 min.
My Tasting Notes
"The blueberry is front and center in the cup — almost jammy, like blueberry compote. As it cools the jasmine really opens up and there's a rich dark chocolate finish. Clean, lingering sweetness. This is one of the best expressions of this bean I've tried. Zero astringency at this grind and temp."

Inverted AeroPress — concentrated & wild fruit bomb
Using immersion to push the natural process character to the absolute limit. Funky, intense, spectacular.
Coffee Type
V60 Pour-over Espresso Americano French Press ☕ AeroPress Chemex
Tools Used
⚙️1Zpresso JX-Pro 🌀AeroPress Go ⚖️Timemore Black Mirror 💧Bonavita Kettle
Brewing Process
1
Dose & Ratio
17g coffee / 220g water — 1:13 ratio for a denser, more syrupy cup. Inverted setup from the start.
2
Grind
Medium (28 clicks JX-Pro). Slightly coarser than V60 since immersion extracts more efficiently.
3
Water Temperature
88°C / 190°F. Lower temp prevents over-extraction in the extended immersion and keeps sweetness clean.
4
Steep
Add all water, stir 10 times gently. Cap and steep 2 minutes total. Flip at 1:45 and press slowly over 30 seconds.
5
Serve
Serve directly or dilute with 50g hot water for a longer drink. Let rest 1 min — the aromatics evolve beautifully as it cools.
My Tasting Notes
"Absolute candy shop in a cup. The immersion amplifies that natural process wildness — super concentrated blueberry, almost wine-like. The bergamot comes through as a clean citrus finish. Full-bodied, syrupy mouthfeel. Not for everyone but spectacular if you love naturals."

Chemex for a group — gentle, clean and crowd-pleasing
Scaled up for 4 cups. The Chemex filter calms the wildness of the natural process and gives an incredibly clean floral cup.
Coffee Type
V60 Pour-over Espresso Americano French Press AeroPress ☕ Chemex
Tools Used
⚙️Baratza Encore ESP 🏺Chemex 6-Cup ⚖️Hario V60 Drip Scale 💧Fellow Stagg EKG
Brewing Process
1
Dose & Ratio
42g coffee / 700g water — 1:16.7 ratio scaled for 4 cups. Use a double-bonded Chemex filter for extra clarity.
2
Grind
Medium-coarse (Baratza Encore setting 22). The thick Chemex filter requires a coarser grind to avoid over-extraction.
3
Water Temperature
93°C / 200°F. Slightly lower than V60 — the longer contact time compensates.
4
Bloom & Pour
Bloom with 80g for 45s. Then pour continuously in slow spirals over 4:00 total. Target drawdown by 5:30 minutes.
My Tasting Notes
"The Chemex strips back some of the intense fruit wildness and leaves a beautifully transparent, floral cup. Jasmine-forward at first sip, clean dark chocolate on the finish. Great for sharing with people who might be surprised by a more intense natural process."
