V60

Absolutely stunning cup. The blueberry notes are pronounced and genuine — not that artificial fruit-forward thing some naturals can get. Brewed as a pourover at 93°C and it sang. The jasmine comes through in the finish in the most delicate way. One of the best Ethiopians I've had this year, full stop.

Chemex

Really impressive. I got the dark chocolate notes on the front, then a wave of dried stone fruit in the mid-palate. Slightly high acidity for my usual preference but I'd absolutely buy again — especially for slow mornings when I want something complex. The 8-day rest from roast date was the sweet spot for me.

Aeropress

I've had the Bishan Beke from two other roasters and Onyx's interpretation is my favourite. The natural process really shines here — super clean despite the fermented sweetness. Brewed Aeropress inverted at a 1:14 ratio and got what felt like warm berry juice with a caramel finish. Exceptional transparency from Onyx as always.
