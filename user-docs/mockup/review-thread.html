Brewed with: Hario V60 · 1:16 ratio · 93°C

This is genuinely one of the most expressive Ethiopians I've had in years. On the first pour, a wave of fresh blueberry and jasmine hit me immediately — not the diffuse, hazy blueberry you often get with natural-processed Ethiopian, but something sharper and more precise. As it cooled, lemon curd and dark chocolate emerged in the finish. The clarity is extraordinary. If you love florals and fruit with real structure, this is required drinking.

Brewed with: Chemex · 1:15 ratio · 91°C

The bergamot note is what sets this apart from every other washed Ethiopian I've tried this year. There's something almost Earl Grey–like about the aromatic profile — deeply floral and tea-forward but with a fruit backbone that keeps things from feeling too delicate. At room temperature, the dark chocolate becomes the dominant note and it's genuinely lovely. The one tiny quibble: a touch high for my wallet, but honestly worth it for a special weekend brew.
